烏龜鍵盤 － 廣東話 (Turtle Keyboard) brings together Cantonese Quick (速成), Cantonese handwriting, English QWERTY typing, and offline Cantonese voice input in one clean, privacy-focused keyboard.

Key features:
• Mixed Cantonese & English Quick (速成): Type Chinese and English seamlessly on the same Quick 3 or Quick 5 keyboard, with multi-word phrase ranking and optional on-device personal learning.
• Cantonese Handwriting: Write Traditional Chinese characters and Hong Kong Cantonese characters with fast on-device recognition and next-word predictions.
• Smart English Typing: Contextual autocorrect, next-word suggestions, auto-capitalization, and a full emoji picker with search and skin-tone variants.
• Offline Voice Input: Speak Cantonese and English to type on any layout, even offline. The speech model downloads over Wi-Fi with no data limit only when you choose to enable voice typing.
• Privacy by Design: Typing predictions, learned Quick selections, handwriting recognition, and voice transcription all run locally on your device.
