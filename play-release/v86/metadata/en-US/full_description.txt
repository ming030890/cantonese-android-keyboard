烏龜鍵盤 － 廣東話 (Turtle Keyboard) is a privacy-first AI Cantonese keyboard built for everyday Hong Kong bilingual typing. We never log or send what you type to any server — every AI model runs locally on your device.

All supported input methods & smart features:
• Offline AI Voice Input (Cantonese & English): Speak Cantonese and English code-switching naturally on any layout, even offline. Powered on-device by the open-source Qwen3-ASR-1.7B-Cantonese-HK speech model (based on Qwen/Qwen3-ASR-1.7B) with Silero VAD. Voice typing is optional and downloads (~2 GB) over Wi-Fi with no data limit only when enabled.
• Cantonese Quick (速成 3 & Quick 5): Type Traditional Chinese and English seamlessly on the same Quick keyboard, with multi-word phrase ranking, English swipe typing directly on Quick, and optional on-device selection learning.
• Cantonese & Traditional Chinese Handwriting: Write Hong Kong Cantonese and Traditional Chinese characters with live per-stroke on-device recognition, Spacebar quick-confirm, visual alternatives, and next-word predictions.
• Smart English QWERTY & Swipe Typing: Smooth English swipe typing, contextual AI autocorrect powered by our compact on-device language model, next-word predictions, model casing restoration, sentence auto-capitalization, and smart punctuation.
• Dedicated Numpad & Form Completions: Automatic 4-column numeric keypad for number, phone, PIN, money, and date/time fields, plus local email (@domain) and recent form-field suggestions.
• Emoji Picker & Clipboard Paste: Full emoji picker with English keyword search, recent emoji, and skin-tone variants, plus local one-tap clipboard paste chips and Android inline autofill.

Privacy-First by Design:
• We never log or send what you type, write, or speak to any server.
• All AI inference (speech recognition, handwriting recognition, language model predictions, and autocorrect) runs locally on your device.
• Cloud backup is disabled, and you can clear learned Quick history anytime in Settings.
