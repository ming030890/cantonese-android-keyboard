烏龜鍵盤 － 廣東話 (Turtle Keyboard) brings together Cantonese Quick (速成), Cantonese handwriting, English QWERTY and swipe typing, and offline Cantonese voice input in one clean, privacy-focused keyboard.

Key features:
• Mixed Cantonese & English Quick (速成): Type Chinese and English seamlessly on the same Quick 3 or Quick 5 keyboard, with swipe typing, multi-word phrase ranking, and optional on-device personal learning.
• Cantonese Handwriting: Write Traditional Chinese characters and Hong Kong Cantonese characters with fast live per-stroke recognition and next-word predictions.
• Smart English & Numeric Typing: Contextual autocorrect, swipe typing, next-word suggestions, auto-capitalization, dedicated numeric keypad, and a full emoji picker with search and skin-tone variants.
• Offline Voice Input: Speak Cantonese and English to type on any layout, even offline, powered on-device by the open-source Qwen3-ASR-1.7B-Cantonese-HK speech model (based on Qwen/Qwen3-ASR-1.7B). The model downloads over Wi-Fi with no data limit only when you choose to enable voice typing.
• Privacy by Design: We never log or send what you type to any server. Typing predictions, learned Quick selections, handwriting recognition, and voice transcription all run locally on your device.
