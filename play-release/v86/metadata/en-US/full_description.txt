A privacy-first AI Cantonese keyboard for everyday Hong Kong typing. Everything runs on your device — we never log or send what you type to any server.

Input methods & features:
• Offline AI Voice: Speak Cantonese and English mixed naturally. Powered on-device by open-source Qwen3-ASR-1.7B-Cantonese-HK (based on Qwen/Qwen3-ASR-1.7B). Downloads (~2 GB) over unlimited Wi-Fi only if enabled.
• Cantonese Quick (速成 3 & 5): Type Chinese and English on one keyboard, with phrase ranking, English swipe typing, and optional local word learning.
• Handwriting: Live per-stroke Cantonese & Traditional Chinese recognition with Spacebar confirm and next-word suggestions.
• Smart English: Swipe typing, AI autocorrect, next-word predictions, auto-capitalization, and smart punctuation.
• Numpad & Helpers: Auto numeric keypad for PINs/numbers, email & form suggestions, clipboard paste, and emoji search with skin tones.

100% on-device. No text logging. Cloud backup disabled.
